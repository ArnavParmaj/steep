# Phased Roadmap (DRAFT v0.1)

Each phase ends with AP reviewing on a real phone. No coding starts until AP approves these docs.

**Phase 0: Discovery (docs and spikes)**
Approve PRD and specs. Agree design direction (reference apps, look and feel). Technical spike: Expo dev build on AP's iPhone, background timer, notification, Live Activity on free account. Confirm grinder baselines by brewing.

**Phase 1: Engine and content**
Recipe engine with unit tests; French press and Moka recipes as data; dial-in rules; milk steps. Validate numbers against real brews.

**Phase 2: Core brew flow**
Home, brew setup, guided timer with why-text, alerts, Live Activity, Android notification.

**Phase 3: Log and learn**
Rating, fix-it suggestions, brew log, brew again, favourites, grinder calibration.

**Phase 4: Beans and safety net**
Beans with freshness, data export and import, recipe duplicate and edit, recipe import/export.

**Phase 5: Polish and ship**
Visual design pass, sounds and haptics, accessibility basics, Android APK, install on friends' phones, feedback.

**After v1**
Brew comparison, water notes, more brewers (AeroPress, V60), possible paid Apple account / TestFlight for easier iPhone sharing.

Rough sizing is deferred until the Phase 0 spike results are known.
