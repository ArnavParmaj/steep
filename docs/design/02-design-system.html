Dark Roast

Design system for the coffee app. Soft, warm and quiet, built to be read from across a kitchen. Two themes only: Espresso (dark) and Cream (light), plus Auto, which follows the phone. Use the toggle to preview both.

Principles

One thing at a timeEach screen has a single focus: the next brew, the running timer, the rating.
Numbers are heroesTime, grams and millilitres are set large and light, so they read from 2 metres away.
Soft, never sharpRound corners, no hard borders, low-contrast surfaces, slow gentle motion.
Explain the whyEvery timer step shows one short sentence on why it matters.

Colour

Eight roles per theme. Components only use roles, never raw hex, so a theme is one small table.

Type

One family, Outfit, in weights from 200 to 600. Thin weights are for big numerals only. Everything under 24 px uses 300 or 400 for body and 500 for emphasis.

Space, shape, depth

Spacing4-point steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48. Screen side margin 22.
RadiusChips and buttons: fully round. Cards: 26. Inner tiles: 20. Never below 12.
DepthNo hard borders. Layers are shown by surface colour (bg, surf, surf2). Only the primary button has a soft amber glow in Espresso.
Touch targetsAt least 48 pt. Main actions 54 pt, so they are easy with wet hands.

Timer ring. It empties slowly, step by step, and the number inside is the only text. Step name and the why sit below it.

Motion

Default easing240 ms ease-out. Screens fade and rise 12 px. No bounces or springs that overshoot.
Timer ringLinear and continuous, updated every frame. Never jumps.
Step changeNumber and step name cross-fade over 400 ms while the ring gently pulses once.
Live dotA slow 2.4 s breathing dot shows the timer is running.
Reduce MotionAll animation drops to simple fades.

Sound and haptics

Two sound sets only, to keep the work small. The phone's silent switch is always respected, and haptics still work in silent mode.

Wood (default)Soft, low wooden tap, like a spoon on a cup.
GlassLight, short glass chime.
Next stepOne sound plus a light tap. Heads-up 10 s before, a quiet single tick.
Brew doneA slightly longer two-note sound plus a soft success haptic.
OffHaptics only, selectable in Settings.
